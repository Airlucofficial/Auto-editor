AutoEditor v2  —  runs entirely on your PC with AI Captions & Transcription

WHAT IT DOES
  1. Sync timestamp-named images + a voiceover into an MP4 video.
  2. Audio-to-Transcript Engine (Local Whisper AI):
     - Automatic high-accuracy speech-to-text with exact word timestamps.
     - Clean second-based interval formatting (e.g. 0.0s – 3.4s).
     - 1-Click Export to TXT and PDF documents with visual timestamp badges.
  3. Automatic Caption Generator (CapCut-Style & In-Editor Preview):
     - 64 curated preset styles across 8 categories (Viral Shorts, Hormozi,
       Neon Glow, Cinematic, Boxed & Pill, Headline 3D, Comic Pop, Broadcast).
     - In-Editor 1-Click "Create Automatic Captions": instantly transcribes your
       voiceover and renders captions directly onto the video canvas while
       editing and scrubbing BEFORE rendering!
     - In-Editor Caption Preset Selector to switch styles seamlessly on the fly.
     - Subtitle burn-in via FFmpeg drawtext / ASS filters for final MP4 export.

HOW TO START
  Method 1 (Recommended - 1-Click AI Launcher):
    - Double-click "Start-AutoEditor-AI.bat"
    - Starts the persistent AI Engine background service and opens AutoEditor.

  Method 2 (Manual Terminal):
    - Run: $env:RENDER_ZOOM_SS = "1"; .\AutoEditor.exe
    - Open http://localhost:4000 in your browser.

REQUIREMENTS
  - Windows 10/11 (64-bit).
  - All transcription, rendering, and encoding run 100% locally on your device.
    Zero cloud uploads, private and offline-capable.

WHERE YOUR VIDEOS GO
  - Finished renders download directly in your browser and are auto-saved to:
    <your Downloads>\AutoEditor\

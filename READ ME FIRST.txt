AutoEditor  —  runs entirely on your PC

WHAT IT DOES
  Turn timestamp-named images + a voiceover into an MP4. Rendering happens on
  YOUR computer using your GPU/CPU. Nothing is uploaded to the internet.

REQUIREMENTS
  Windows 10/11 (64-bit). Nothing to install — everything (the app engine and
  the video encoder) is bundled inside this folder.

HOW TO START
  1. Double-click  AutoEditor.exe
  2. A black window opens and says the app is running. Your browser opens
     automatically to  http://localhost:4000
  3. KEEP THAT BLACK WINDOW OPEN while you use the app.

HOW TO STOP
  - Close the black window, or press Ctrl+C in it.

WHERE YOUR VIDEOS GO
  - Each finished render downloads through your browser as usual, AND is also
    auto-saved to:  <your Downloads>\AutoEditor\
    So even if you refresh or close the tab right as it finishes, the MP4 is
    safely on disk. The black window prints the exact folder on startup.

NOTES
  - First launch takes a few seconds while it detects your graphics hardware.
  - A dedicated/integrated GPU (Intel/NVIDIA/AMD) is used automatically for a
    big speed-up; if none works, it falls back to the CPU and still renders.
  - If Windows SmartScreen warns about AutoEditor.exe, choose "More info" ->
    "Run anyway" (it's just the bundled app, unsigned).
  - Don't delete AutoEditor.exe, ffmpeg.exe, caption.ttf, or the "out" folder
    - the app needs them.
